# Vengerberg City Guide
## The Pearl of the Northern Kingdoms

---

## Overview

Vengerberg is a magnificent city located in the Northern Kingdoms, a hub of commerce, culture, and political power. The city sits along the banks of the mighty Pontar River, which serves as both a natural defense and a vital trade route.

The city is protected by high stone walls and guarded gates, with five distinct wards serving different functions in city life. Beyond the walls lie the northern and western forests, fertile southern farmlands, and the eastern riverbanks.

**Total Size**: 32x32 grid (1,024 rooms)
**Connections**: 3,968 exits (all bidirectional)
**Founded**: Ancient times, predating many modern kingdoms

## The Five Wards

### 1. Temple District (Northwest City)
**Grid Location**: Rows 11-16, Columns 8-15
**Character**: Sacred and contemplative

The spiritual heart of Vengerberg, the Temple District is dominated by magnificent religious structures dedicated to various deities. The Great Temple of Melitele stands as the crown jewel, its white marble columns visible from across the city.

**Key Features**:
- **The Great Temple of Melitele** (13, 11) - Main religious center, healing services
- Sacred gardens and meditation areas
- Monasteries and shrines to various deities
- Priests and priestesses offering blessings
- Pilgrims from across the land
- Peaceful atmosphere, incense-scented air

**Streets**: Prayer Street, Pilgrim Way, Temple Avenue, Sacred Path, Devotion Lane

**Services Available**:
- Healing and divine magic
- Blessings and religious counsel
- Marriage ceremonies
- Funeral rites
- Sanctuary for those in need

---

### 2. Royal Quarter (Northeast City)
**Grid Location**: Rows 11-16, Columns 16-25
**Character**: Wealthy and exclusive

Home to Vengerberg's nobility and wealthy elite, the Royal Quarter features grand estates, manicured gardens, and the Royal Palace itself. Heavy guard presence ensures the safety of the privileged residents.

**Key Features**:
- **Royal Palace** (13, 21) - Seat of government and power
- Noble estates with private grounds
- Private parks and gardens
- Elite social gatherings
- High security and armed guards
- Immaculately maintained streets

**Streets**: Noble Avenue, Crown Street, Palace Road, Aristocrat Way, Royal Boulevard

**Access**: Many areas restricted to nobility or those with official business

---

### 3. Market Ward (Central City)
**Grid Location**: Rows 17-20, Columns 8-25
**Character**: Bustling and vibrant

The beating heart of Vengerberg's economy, the Market Ward is a maze of stalls, shops, taverns, and businesses. Day and night, merchants hawk their wares while crowds press through the streets.

**Key Features**:
- **Central Market Square** (18, 16) - Main marketplace, DEFAULT HOME LOCATION
- **The Prancing Pony Tavern** (18, 14) - Popular inn and tavern
- Hundreds of merchant stalls
- Weaponsmiths, armorers, general goods
- Food vendors and restaurants
- Street performers and entertainers
- Pickpockets and petty criminals

**Streets**: Merchant Street, Trade Avenue, Market Row, Commerce Lane, Bazaar Way

**Services Available**:
- Buying and selling all manner of goods
- Lodging at various taverns and inns
- Food and drink
- Information gathering (for a price)
- Haggling and deal-making

**Note**: This is the default starting location for new players (Central Market Square)

---

### 4. Craftsmen Quarter (Southwest City)
**Grid Location**: Rows 21-23, Columns 8-16
**Character**: Industrial and skilled

The working heart of the city, the Craftsmen Quarter rings with the sounds of hammers on anvils, saws cutting wood, and the bustle of productive industry. Master artisans create goods of exceptional quality here.

**Key Features**:
- **The Master Forge** (22, 12) - Legendary Dwarven smithy
- Blacksmith forges and armories
- Leatherworkers and tanners
- Carpenters and woodworkers
- Jewelcrafters and gem-setters
- Apprentices learning trades
- Hot, smoky atmosphere

**Streets**: Artisan Street, Smith Row, Craftsman Lane, Workshop Way, Forge Avenue

**Services Available**:
- Custom weapon and armor crafting
- Repair services
- Masterwork items (expensive)
- Apprenticeship opportunities
- Raw materials for crafting

**Notable NPCs**:
- **Yarpen** - Master dwarf smith at the Master Forge

---

### 5. River Ward (Southeast City)
**Grid Location**: Rows 21-23, Columns 17-25
**Character**: Rough and utilitarian

Sitting along the Pontar River, the River Ward is where ships dock, goods are loaded and unloaded, and sailors from distant lands swap tales. It's rougher than the rest of the city, but vital to commerce.

**Key Features**:
- **Harbor Master's Office** (22, 22) - Trade administration
- **The Rusty Anchor Inn** (22, 19) - Sailors' tavern
- Multiple docks and wharves
- Warehouses storing trade goods
- Fishermen and their catches
- River barges and trade vessels
- Longshoremen and dock workers

**Streets**: Dock Street, Harbor Way, Wharf Lane, Sailor Row, Quay Avenue

**Services Available**:
- River transport and trade
- Fresh fish and seafood
- Cheap lodging (The Rusty Anchor)
- Cargo storage
- Rumors from foreign lands

## Surrounding Areas

### Northern Woods (Rows 0-5, All Columns)
**Character**: Natural wilderness, hunting grounds

Dense forest stretching north of the city. Good for hunting, gathering herbs, and encountering wildlife. Home to a hidden druid grove.

**Dangers**: Wild animals, getting lost
**Resources**: Game animals, herbs, wood
**Notable Location**: Hidden Druid Grove (2, 15) - Sacred circle of standing stones

---

### Western Woods (Rows 6-23, Columns 0-5)
**Character**: Dark and dangerous

More sinister than the northern woods, these twisted forests are known to harbor bandits and worse. Travelers are advised to exercise extreme caution.

**Dangers**: Bandits, dark creatures, getting lost
**Resources**: Timber (if you survive)
**Reputation**: Avoid after dark

---

### Iron Mine (Rows 6-10, Columns 6-11)
**Character**: Industrial, productive

An active iron mine supplying Vengerberg's smiths with raw ore. Miners work dangerous shifts extracting the valuable metal.

**Key Location**: Main Shaft (7, 8) - Primary mine entrance
**Employment**: Miners always needed (dangerous work)
**Products**: Iron ore, occasional gems
**Conditions**: Dark, dangerous, sulfurous

---

### Northern Approach (Rows 6-10, Columns 12-25)
**Character**: Well-traveled trade route

The main road leading north from Vengerberg. Well-maintained and patrolled, this is the safest approach to the city.

**Features**:
- Wide, maintained road
- Guard patrols
- Roadside inns
- Merchant caravans
- Distance markers

**Connects To**: Northern Gate (10, 16)

---

### Western Approach (Rows 11-23, Columns 6-7)
**Character**: Less traveled, more dangerous

A secondary route into the city from the west, skirting the edge of the dangerous western woods.

**Features**:
- Poor road condition
- Warning signs about bandits
- Light traffic
- Vigilance required

---

### Pontar River (Rows 6-31, Columns 26-31)
**Character**: Vital waterway

The mighty Pontar River forms Vengerberg's eastern border, providing water, trade, and fish. Strong currents make swimming dangerous.

**Features**:
- Swift, deep waters
- Trade barges and fishing boats
- Reeds and rushes along banks
- Rich fishing
- Some areas suitable for docks, others wild

**Danger**: Strong currents, drowning risk

---

### Southern Farmlands (Rows 24-31, Columns 0-25)
**Character**: Productive agricultural land

Fertile fields and small farming villages provide food for Vengerberg's population. Peaceful rural landscapes contrast with urban intensity.

**Features**:
- Wheat and barley fields
- Farmhouses and villages
- Windmills and granaries
- Sheep and cattle
- Village markets
- Hardworking farmers

**Products**: Grain, livestock, produce

## Key Locations & Landmarks

### 1. Northern Gate (10, 16) - Room #339
**The Main Entrance to Vengerberg**

Massive wooden doors reinforced with iron bands stand ready to seal the city in times of danger. Guards in Aedirn's livery scrutinize all who enter. Above, the city's coat of arms is carved in stone. This is the primary entry point for travelers approaching from the north.

**Function**: Main city gate
**Guards**: Always present
**Access**: Open during day, controlled at night

---

### 2. Great Temple of Melitele (13, 11) - Room #430
**The Largest Religious Structure**

White marble columns support a soaring dome, with stained glass casting colored light across the interior. Priestesses in white robes offer healing and counsel. The air is thick with incense and murmured prayers. This is the spiritual center of Vengerberg.

**Services**:
- Healing (physical and spiritual)
- Blessings
- Religious counsel
- Sanctuary
- Marriage and funeral rites

**Priestesses**: Available for consultation
**Atmosphere**: Peaceful, sacred

---

### 3. Royal Palace (13, 21) - Room #440
**Seat of Power**

A masterwork of architecture with soaring spires and elegant stonework. Immaculately maintained gardens surround the palace. Guards in ceremonial armor stand at every entrance, halberds gleaming. Only those with official business are permitted entry.

**Function**: Government seat
**Access**: Restricted (official business only)
**Security**: Heavy guard presence
**Architecture**: Magnificent, impressive

---

### 4. Central Market Square (18, 16) - Room #595
**The Economic Heart - DEFAULT HOME**

Hundreds of stalls crowd the large plaza, merchants selling everything from food to weapons to magical talismans. The noise is deafening: vendors shouting, customers haggling, animals protesting. Smells of spices, sweat, livestock, and cooking food mingle. A large fountain provides drinking water in the square's center.

**Function**: Main marketplace
**DEFAULT HOME**: New characters start here
**Services**: Everything imaginable for sale
**Atmosphere**: Loud, crowded, vibrant
**Pickpockets**: Watch your coinpurse!

**Activities Available**:
- Shopping (all types)
- Haggling
- Information gathering
- People watching
- Finding employment

### 5. The Prancing Pony Tavern (18, 14) - Room #???
**Popular Tavern and Inn**

One of Vengerberg's most popular taverns, featuring a large common room with roaring fireplace, rough wooden tables, and a long bar. Ale flows freely, food is simple but hearty. Travelers, merchants, and locals mix, swapping gossip and stories. A bard plays in the corner.

**Services**:
- Food and drink
- Lodging (rooms upstairs)
- Entertainment (bard)
- Social gathering
- Rumors and information

**Atmosphere**: Lively, welcoming
**Clientele**: Mixed (all types)
**Reputation**: Safe, friendly

---

### 6. The Master Forge (22, 12) - Room #719
**Legendary Dwarven Smithy**

Run by Dwarven smiths whose skill is legendary throughout the realm. The heat from the massive forge is intense, molten metal glowing. Weapons and armor of extraordinary quality hang on display. Master smith Yarpen, a grizzled dwarf, oversees every piece personally.

**Services**:
- Custom weapon crafting
- Custom armor crafting
- Masterwork items
- Repairs (any quality)
- Weapon/armor modification

**Master Smith**: Yarpen (dwarf, legendary skill)
**Specialty**: Exceptional quality, fair prices
**Reputation**: Best smithy in the city

**Prices**: High quality commands high prices

---

### 7. Harbor Master's Office (22, 22) - Room #729
**River Trade Administration**

Overlooking the main docks, this weathered building of stone and timber serves as the administrative center for all river trade. The harbor master keeps ledgers of every ship, cargo manifest, and toll collected. Maps of the Pontar River cover the walls, marked with hazards and trade routes.

**Function**: Trade regulation
**Services**:
- Ship registration
- Cargo manifests
- Trade information
- River navigation info
- Toll collection

**Harbor Master**: Available for consultation
**Records**: Meticulous ledgers of all trade

---

### 8. Iron Mine - Main Shaft (7, 8) - Room #235
**Primary Mine Entrance**

The main shaft descends deep into the earth. Torches light the entrance, but darkness swallows the tunnel within. Miners trudge in and out, faces blackened with dust. The sound of pickaxes striking stone echoes from below. A foreman checks every worker, ensuring production quotas are met.

**Function**: Iron ore extraction
**Employment**: Miners needed (dangerous)
**Products**: Iron ore, occasional gems
**Conditions**: Dark, hot, dangerous
**Management**: Strict foreman oversight

---

### 9. Hidden Druid Grove (2, 15) - Room #82
**Sacred Natural Site**

A secret grove hidden deep in the northern woods. Ancient standing stones form a circle, covered in moss and carved with runic symbols. Druids who tend this place worship the old gods, far from the city's temples. The air feels charged with primal magic.

**Function**: Druid worship site
**Access**: Hidden, must be found
**Atmosphere**: Mystical, ancient
**Druids**: Present, secretive
**Magic**: Primal forces strong here

---

### 10. The Rusty Anchor Inn (22, 19) - Room #???
**Sailors' Tavern**

Catering to sailors and dock workers, this cramped and smoky establishment smells of fish, sweat, and cheap alcohol. Despite its rough atmosphere, the Anchor is known for having the best fish stew in the city. Sailors from distant ports trade tales of adventure and danger.

**Services**:
- Cheap drinks
- Best fish stew in city
- Lodging (basic)

**Atmosphere**: Rough, authentic
**Clientele**: Sailors, dock workers

## Navigation Guide

### Basic Movement

Vengerberg uses a grid system with cardinal direction exits:
- **north** / **n** - Move north (decrease row number)
- **south** / **s** - Move south (increase row number)
- **east** / **e** - Move east (increase column number)
- **west** / **w** - Move west (decrease column number)

All rooms are connected to adjacent rooms (except at grid edges).

### Finding Locations

To find a specific location, note its coordinates (row, column):

Example: To reach Central Market Square (18, 16) from Northern Gate (10, 16):
1. From Northern Gate, go south 8 times (10 → 18)
2. You're now at Central Market Square

Example: To reach Royal Palace (13, 21) from Central Market (18, 16):
1. Go north 5 times (18 → 13)
2. Go east 5 times (16 → 21)
3. You're now at the Royal Palace

### Room Information

Each room displays:
- Room name (e.g., "Central Market Square")
- Description (what you see)
- Exits available (north, south, east, west)
- Objects in the room
- Characters present

Use **look** or **l** to see the room description again.

## Roleplaying in Vengerberg

### Ward-Specific Behavior

**Temple District**:
- Speak quietly and respectfully
- Remove weapons in sacred spaces
- Offer donations at shrines
- Seek spiritual guidance

**Royal Quarter**:
- Dress appropriately (no rags)
- Show deference to nobility
- Guards will question suspicious behavior
- Private property strictly enforced

**Market Ward**:
- Haggle expected and encouraged
- Watch for pickpockets
- Loud and boisterous atmosphere welcomed
- All manner of folk mix freely

**Craftsmen Quarter**:
- Respect the work being done
- Don't interrupt craftsmen mid-task
- Expect noise and heat
- Apprentices may run errands

**River Ward**:
- Rougher language acceptable
- Sailor stories and boasts common
- Keep your wits about you
- Fish smell is unavoidable

### Local Customs

- **Coins**: Crowns are the standard currency
- **Greetings**: Nod or verbal greeting appropriate
- **Nobility**: Address as "Lord" or "Lady"
- **Guards**: Obey promptly, don't argue
- **Temples**: Always show respect
- **Taverns**: Buy a drink or leave
- **Markets**: Haggling is expected

### Safety Tips

1. **Temple District**: Safest, well-lit, guards nearby
2. **Royal Quarter**: Safe but restricted access
3. **Market Ward**: Watch for pickpockets
4. **Craftsmen Quarter**: Fire hazards, heavy loads
5. **River Ward**: Rougher element, stay alert
6. **Northern Woods**: Hunting ground, bring weapons
7. **Western Woods**: DANGEROUS - avoid alone
8. **Iron Mine**: Cave-ins, toxic air, foreman strict

## Quick Start for New Players

1. **Login** and create your character
2. **Start Location**: You begin at Central Market Square (18, 16)
3. **Explore**: Use `north`, `south`, `east`, `west` to move
4. **Shop**: Browse Market Ward for starting equipment
5. **Craft**: Visit Craftsmen Quarter for weapons/armor
6. **Train**: Temple District for healing, Royal Quarter for quests
7. **Socialize**: Taverns like The Prancing Pony (18, 14)
8. **Adventure**: Northern woods for hunting, southern farms for jobs

### Recommended First Steps

1. **Look around**: `look` to read room descriptions
2. **Check inventory**: `inventory` to see what you have
3. **Visit a shop**: Browse Market Ward shops
4. **Find a tavern**: The Prancing Pony (2 west from market)
5. **Talk to NPCs**: `talk <name>` to learn about quests
6. **Join an organization**: Check available factions
7. **Start small**: Take on simple quests first
