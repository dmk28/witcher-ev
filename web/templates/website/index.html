Welcome to the Northern Kingdoms

⚔️

Deep Combat System

Engage in strategic turn-based combat with multiple weapon types, stances, and tactical options. Master the blade, axe, or magic to overcome your foes.

👑

Political Intrigue

Navigate the treacherous waters of court politics with our social combat system. Negotiate, seduce, intimidate, or deceive your way to power.

🛡️

Rich Character System

Choose from diverse vocations including Witchers, Mages, Knights, and Artisans. Develop your skills and abilities through a flexible advancement system.

⚒️

Crafting & Economy

Master the art of crafting legendary weapons and armor. Trade in bustling markets, haggle with merchants, and build your fortune across the kingdoms.

🏰

Organizations & Factions

Join noble houses, guilds, and secret societies. Rise through the ranks, manage organizations, and shape the political landscape of the North.

📜

Epic Storytelling

Participate in GM-run missions and player-driven stories. Your choices matter in a living world that responds to player actions.

Choose Your Path

⚔️ Witcher

Mutated monster hunters with supernatural abilities and mastery of combat and alchemy.

🔮 Mage

Wielders of powerful magic, advisors to kings, and masters of arcane knowledge.

🛡️ Knight

Noble warriors bound by honor, skilled in mounted combat and the art of war.

🗡️ Mercenary

Battle-hardened sellswords who fight for coin and glory across the war-torn lands.

🎭 Bard

Charismatic performers who inspire allies and manipulate enemies with word and song.

⚒️ Artisan

Master craftspeople who create legendary weapons, armor, and magical items.

🏹 Ranger

Wilderness scouts expert in tracking, survival, and archery.

🗝️ Spy

Shadow operatives who gather intelligence and eliminate targets from the darkness.
